ABOUT BENDSKIN RUSH

Welcome to Bendskin Rush

Bendskin Rush is a 1080p first-person urban motorcycle racing game built for instant play in mobile and desktop browsers. Weave smoothly between sculpted yellow taxis, clando vans, timber trucks, rival riders, and road barriers while chasing your personal best and city leaderboard rank.

How to Ride, Signals, Handedness & Controls

  • Desktop Keyboard: ← / → or A / D steer smoothly; ↑ / W throttle; ↓ / S brake; hold Space for continuous Moto Horn (traffic blinks & yields aside!); Esc or P opens Pause Menu (navigate with ↑ / ↓ and press Enter).
  • Mobile Car Pedals & Left/Right-Handed Swap: Use < / > steering arrows or swipe (switch Left-Handed / Right-Handed in Settings to swap steering arrows and the car-style GAS / BRAKE foot pedals between Left and Right).
  • Vehicle Turn Signals: Watch every car, van, truck, and rival bike flash its amber turn-signal indicator before changing lanes so you can react cleanly at high speed.
  • Per-Account Coins & Full-Bike Garage: Collect spinning Gold Coins (+10) and Sharp Dodges (+1) stored privately per account. Open Garage to inspect every motorcycle in full rotatable 3D!
  • Photorealistic 3D Graphics Ladder: Choose Low 3D (real-time WebGL 3D), Medium 720p+ (supersampled 720p+ 3D cockpit), High 1080p (1080p PBR textures, 16x AF & sculpted meshes), or Ultra Real 1080p+ (true normal-mapped asphalt, sidewalks, guardrails & overhead gantries, real sculpted bike cockpit + anatomical human rider hands & watch, real cars with drivers & interiors, real rival riders & roadside pedestrians) in Settings.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

King of Race Interactive respects your privacy. Bendskin Rush operates with minimal data collection designed for fast, anonymous arcade play.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your browser's localStorage stores your best distance, optional rider nickname, chosen city tag, generated anonymous player ID, control settings (drive mode, steering sensitivity, audio preference), and cached leaderboard standings. You can clear this data anytime via your browser site settings.

2. Leaderboard & Server Logs

When you choose a rider nickname and city tag, your personal best distance, max combo, and run verification telemetry are stored to maintain the public leaderboard. Do not enter personal real names, phone numbers, or sensitive information as your nickname.

3. Advertising, Cookies & Google AdSense

Non-intrusive banner placements on menu/summary screens and optional rewarded continues may use Google AdSense / Google H5 Games Ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this game and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or review how Google uses data at Google Advertising Policies.
